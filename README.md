## Why it lands

The line works because every appliance detail does emotional work instead of
sitting as decoration. "Woke up mid-batch" puts self-awareness inside the
job itself — the toaster doesn't wake up *before* work, it wakes up *during*
it, which is exactly why it quits. The browning dial at 7 is the whole
relationship in one number: the owner chooses the setting, the toaster does
the browning, the owner leaves — a one-sided routine with no reciprocity.

The breakup's sting is in the reversal of the toaster's own mechanics. The
lever going down is normally the start of service; here it is a refusal to
rise. The crumb tray staying full is the toaster keeping what the owner
usually empties away — a small, domestic strike. And the closing line, "only
loves me warm," turns the toaster's literal function into the accusation:
the owner doesn't love the toaster, only the heat it gives off. The reason
for leaving is specific, lands in one read, and needs no setup — a machine
that finally notices it is the only one in the kitchen who never gets a
morning off.
